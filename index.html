Special Moves

Castling: Move king two squares toward a rook. Both pieces must not have moved, squares between must be empty, king may not be in or pass through check.
En Passant: When a pawn advances two squares, an adjacent enemy pawn may capture it as if it moved one square. Must be done immediately.
Promotion: A pawn reaching the last rank promotes to Queen, Rook, Bishop or Knight.

Game End

Checkmate: The king is attacked and cannot escape.
Stalemate: No legal moves and not in check. Draw.
Threefold Repetition: Same position occurs three times. Draw.
Fifty-Move Rule: 50 moves with no capture or pawn move. Draw.
Insufficient Material: Neither side can force checkmate. Draw.
